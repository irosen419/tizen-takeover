# Home Hub live server: notes for Claude on the Pi

This folder runs the server that feeds the Home Hub screen on the owner's TV.
When the owner asks to change what the TV shows, or to add, tick or remove a
to-do, run `./hub ...` from this folder and report the one-line result back.

## Commands (one example each)

- Open a widget: `./hub show weather` (add `--full` for full screen)
- Close whatever is open: `./hub home`
- Short message on the TV: `./hub say "Dinner in 10" --seconds 8`
- Play audio on the TV: `./hub play https://example.com/doorbell.mp3 --title "Doorbell"` (http or https; the hub must be open); stop it: `./hub stop`
- The newest NPR News Now newscast (a five minute summary posted every hour) on the TV: `./hub news` (server 0.21.0, hub v0.37.0; redirects followed and the key-press unlock from server 0.22.3, hub v0.38.2)
- Echo speakers through Home Assistant (server 0.22.0): `./hub echo status`; `./hub echo audible` (resume the book), `./hub echo kera`, `./hub echo news`; `./hub echo start pandora --query "jazz"` (apps: audible, pandora, amazon-music, tunein (needs `--query`), kera, news; a query is up to 100 characters and not for audible, news or kera); `./hub echo pause|resume|next|stop`; `./hub echo volume 40` (0-100, or `up`/`down`); add `--on show|dot|everywhere` (the first target is the default). All sounds then marks it "Started from the hub"; for Audible it shows the book, author and cover when Alexa reports them, else just "Audible" in place of a stale title
- Load the hub afresh: `./hub reload`
- What the TV shows now (layout, what is open, which animations can play there): `./hub screen`; switch the TV's layout as its layout bar does (the Pi's Main stays as saved): `./hub pick budget`; play an animation now: `./hub scene fetch` (ids from `./hub screen`: stroll, fetch, sock-heist, zoomies, perch, morning, evening, night, weather on the home screen; band, singer, encore, outfit in the visualizer; headphones, dance on the expanded Now Playing page); open the visualizer: `./hub visualizer`. Server 0.48.0, hub 0.89.0. Home Assistant's Home Hub dashboard uses the same (`GET /api/screen`, `POST /api/command`). Ask the owner before sending the TV anything
- Add a to-do: `./hub todo add "Buy milk" --level must --due 2026-12-24 --notes "Oat, not dairy"` (level, due and notes are optional; levels are `must`, `optional` (the default) and `passive` (a reminder shown on the TV under "Coming up" from 2 days before its due date); notes are plain text up to 300 characters; `--section` is still taken but no longer shown)
- Change a to-do's level: `./hub todo level "Buy milk" passive` (id or exact title)
- When a passive to-do shows under "Coming up": `./hub todo lead "School meeting" 3` (whole days before its due date, 0 to 30; 0 = only on the day; `--clear` goes back to 2), or `--lead 3` on `./hub todo add`
- See the to-do list with ids and notes: `./hub todo list`
- Set or change a to-do's notes: `./hub todo notes "Buy milk" "Oat, not dairy"` (id or exact title); `--clear` removes them
- Tick a to-do: `./hub todo done "Buy milk"` (id or exact title)
- Remove a to-do: `./hub todo remove "Buy milk"` (id or exact title)
- Grocery list (server 0.26.0; the touch app at `http://<Pi>:8097/` on a phone shows it, and the to-do list too from server 0.29.0): `./hub grocery add "Milk" "Eggs"` (one quoted name per item, up to 20); `./hub grocery list`; tick: `./hub grocery done "Milk"`; `./hub grocery remove "Milk"` (id or name, any case); drop every ticked item: `./hub grocery clear`; what to search a store for (one "Brand Name Size" line per item to get, scanned items carry their product): `./hub grocery export`
- Pantry (server 0.30.0; SQLite `data/pantry.db`, the phone's Pantry view): `./hub pantry add "Eggs" --qty 12 --at 6 --place fridge` (`--brand`, `--auto off` optional; places fridge, freezer, pantry, bathroom, other); `./hub pantry set "Eggs" --qty 2` (id or name; `--at`, `--place`, `--auto on|off`); `./hub pantry list`; `./hub pantry low`. Counts and `--at` are whole numbers. A count that drops to or under its restock point (`--at`) puts the item on the grocery list once by itself (never the add that first puts it in the pantry). Never put a product name, brand or code in a log line
- Barcode products (the touch app's Scan button; `products.js`, catalog in `data/products.json`): a scan looks in the catalog, then Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts and UPCitemdb (100 a day). Manual entries from the phone win. Never put a scanned product's name or code in a log line, a commit or a public file. HTTPS for the camera: `tailscale serve --bg 8097` on the Pi (never `funnel`)
- Calendar to-dos: the Pi itself turns events titled `TODO:` (optional), `TODO!:` (must) and `TODO~:` (passive; a trailing number 0-30 is its lead in days) into to-dos on every calendar refresh, no Claude task (`pi/todoconvert.js`). `TODO:` events (today or tomorrow, not repeating, no guests) are then hidden from the hub calendar. Once the to-do is ticked, the Pi deletes a `TODO:` event in Google (only that day's occurrence) and keeps `TODO!:`/`TODO~:`; `#keep` / `#remove` in the event description override that. Needs the service account key `data/google-calendar-key.json` (README.md, "Calendar to-dos, and deleting ticked events"); never print it. Ledger: `data/todo-converter.json`. Force it now: `./hub calendar refresh`
- Calendars and the next event: `./hub calendar status`
- Read the calendars again now: `./hub calendar refresh`
- Tile layout on the home screen: `./hub layout show`; a new one: `./hub layout set layout.json` (a JSON list of `{"widget":"weather","x":0,"y":0,"w":3,"h":2}` on the 12 x 6 grid; `-` reads it from input); back to the built-in one: `./hub layout reset`
- A World Worth Saving snapshot: `./hub awws show` (when it was sent, its size, one line per section); a new one: `./hub awws set snapshot.json` (the snapshot itself, or `{"snapshot": {...}}`; `-` reads it from input; shape in `docs/awws-snapshot.md`); ask for a sync now: `./hub awws resync` (the routine that reads the board picks it up at its next check, or fire it by hand); remove it: `./hub awws clear`
- Budget snapshot (totals from the budget app on this Pi): `./hub budget show` (dates, counts and states, never amounts or labels); by hand `./hub budget set budget.json` (shape in `docs/budget-snapshot.md`); remove it: `./hub budget clear`; Budget lab's daily spending goal, from today on (the Pi's date; past days keep theirs; whole dollars 1-1,000; the owner normally sets it on the TV): `./hub budget goal 60`, and `./hub budget goal` says when it was last set (never the amount). Kept in `data/budget-goal.json`, merged into the snapshot as `pace.goals`
- What is playing on Spotify, and sign-in: `./hub spotify status`; one-time sign-in `./hub spotify login` (prints a link, then asks for the pasted address) or `./hub spotify code "<address>"`; `./hub spotify logout`
- The room's lights (Ambience, through Home Assistant; server 0.7.0): `./hub ambience status`; the ids: `./hub ambience scenes`; a scene: `./hub ambience scene chill`; one light: `./hub ambience light geeni 40` (or `on`/`off`); the plant wall: `./hub ambience mood dim`; new lights and scenes: `./hub ambience set my-lights.json` (shape in `ambience.example.json`); `./hub ambience reset`
- Music lights (the Govee lamp and bars follow the Pi's music; engine `pi/lights/engine.js`, user unit `music-lights`): `./hub lights` (status); `./hub lights on` / `off`; `./hub lights chill|groove|party`; `./hub lights set --palette sunset --vibrancy low|medium|high --beat low|medium|high --quiet 15|never`; `./hub lights palettes`. Any other way of setting the lights (Ambience scene, Govee app, voice, HA) turns it off and is left alone. Ask the owner before switching it on. Restart only that unit, never the audio services
- Lamp and bars presets (one name sets both Govee lights; README.md, "Lamp and bars presets"): `./hub lights presets`; `./hub lights preset chill-moving` (`off`: none; music lights stop); `./hub lights save "Pink"` (what both show now); `./hub lights add preset.json`; `./hub lights forget my-pink`; `./hub lights snapshots` (Govee app snapshots per light)
- TV shows on the Schedule (Entertainment panel): `./hub shows list`; add one `./hub shows add "Stick" --service "Apple TV"` (`--id 12345` for its TVmaze number when the name finds the wrong show, `--watch Hulu` to open another app than the badge, `--also "Hulu,Peacock"` for other apps that carry it, `--via "Prime Video"` when the service is billed through Prime Video Channels, `--link 80123456` the show's id in its app, kept for later: Watch still only opens the app); `./hub shows remove "Stick"`; ask TVmaze now: `./hub shows refresh` (it also does so every 6 hours); the owner marks episodes watched on the TV himself ("Did you finish?" and the check on a row), so use these only for a mistake: `./hub shows watched "Stick" S3E4` (leave `S3E4` out: the earliest episode out in the last week that is not marked) and `./hub shows unwatch "Stick" S3E4` (server 0.8.0); progress beyond one episode, also normally set on the show page on the TV: `./hub shows upto "Stick" S3E4` (watched up to and including S3E4: everything before it counts, for a binge; a later `upto` with an earlier episode replaces it) and `./hub shows caughtup "Stick"` (every episode out so far), server 0.14.0
- What the TV's watch link test found (which deep links open a title): `./hub linktests`
- The TV's remote driven from the Pi (server 0.23.0, app found by name from 0.24.1; the watch link test's "Open, Enter, then ..." rows use it): `./hub tvremote` (paired or not, the last run's steps); a run by hand: `./hub tvremote run '{"id":"x","appId":"3201907018807","link":"dial","dialApp":"Netflix","payload":"m=..."}'`. The first run makes the TV ask to allow "Home Hub Pi" (Allow on the remote); the token stays in `data/tvremote.json`
- What plays anywhere (Echo speakers through Home Assistant, and Spotify), for Now Playing: `./hub allsounds status` (state, source, speaker, title and artist only)
- Guest mode PIN (server 0.31.0; the TV asks for it to turn Guest mode on or off, the Pi keeps only a hash): `./hub guest-pin status`; forgot it: `./hub guest-pin reset` (the TV then asks to create a new one). Never ask for, print or log a PIN. Not in the mailbox
- Photo album (friends' photos copied from the wall calendar every 30 minutes by `photos-sync.sh` on the Pi host; README.md, "Photo album"): `./hub photos status` (how many photos, the last sync, ok or not, how many new). Never put a photo's file name or id in a log line, a commit or a reply
- Is the server up, which screens are on: `./hub status`
- The auto-pull timer (server 0.32.0, `autopull/`): the Pi pulls `main` every 5 minutes and restarts the hub (then the budget app) only when a server file in `pi/` changed. `hubauto` shows its last status, log lines and last error (`~/.local/state/tizen-takeover/autopull/autopull.log`, counts only; `pi/data` is root-owned so nothing is written there); `hubauto off|on` pauses it (`autopull.off` in that folder; the old `pi/data/autopull.off` still works). `GET /health` and `/api/status` carry `app`, the hub version the Pi serves
- Is the Claude mailbox (Cloudflare R2) connected: `./hub mailbox status`
- The server's recent log lines: `./hub log` (last 50); only problems: `./hub log --errors`; more: `./hub log --lines 120` (1 to 200)
- All commands: `./hub help`

Widget ids for `./hub show` are `weather`, `todo`, `calendar`, `entertainment`, `nowplaying`, `today` (the clock), `awws`, `awws-visits`, `awws-agenda`, `awws-todo`, `awws-reach`, `awws-money` (Earned vs spent), `budget-room` (Daily room), `budget-card` (Card day), `budget-goals` (Goals), `budget-lab` (Budget lab: the pace streak and the cap slider from hub v0.29.3, the merchants' cuts and Put it somewhere from v0.29.4; the runway chart to card day at full screen from v0.29.5; an older hub ignores the id) and `soundctl` (Sound control, a test widget: All sounds plus Echo buttons; hub v0.39.0), `allsounds` (All sounds, a test widget: what plays on any speaker; only on the "All sounds test" layout, from v0.28.16). The layout switcher on the TV has an "AWWS money" and a "Budget" layout with these. A layout may also place the other tiles in `HUB_CONFIG.layout` (`app/config.js`), such as `today`; run `./hub layout show` first and change only what was asked.

## From the cloud: the Claude mailbox

A Claude thread in the cloud cannot reach the Pi. It sends the same commands through the Cloudflare R2 bucket `home-hub-mailbox`; the Pi checks it every 10 seconds (setup: README.md, "Claude mailbox"). Use curl through the proxy (no key needed on your side; the account id is not secret). The Cloudflare MCP `execute` tool gets 403 on R2 objects, so do not use it for this.

```sh
B=https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/f24d1741cf57727291b631def49ffacb/r2/buckets/home-hub-mailbox/objects

# 1. Is the Pi alive? "updated" should be under ~2 minutes old.
curl -sS "$B/status.json"

# 2. Send a command. The id is 6-64 of A-Z a-z 0-9 _ - and is also the file name.
ID=c$(date +%s)$RANDOM
printf '{"id":"%s","created":"%s","cmd":"todo.add","args":{"title":"Buy milk","section":"now"}}' \
  "$ID" "$(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)" > cmd.json
curl -sS -X PUT "$B/inbox%2F$ID.json" -H 'content-type: application/json' --data @cmd.json

# 3. Wait about 10-20 seconds, then read the reply (an error JSON means "not there yet").
curl -sS "$B/outbox%2F$ID.json"
```

Write `cmd.json` in your scratchpad folder. The reply is `{"id", "cmd", "ok", "state", "message", "data", "started", "finished"}`. Wait until `"state": "done"` (`"running"` means it is still going; `calendar.refresh` can take 25 seconds). Report `message` to the owner: it is the same text `./hub` prints. `ok: false` with `Rejected: ...` means it was never run; fix what it says and send a new id. No reply after a minute: check `status.json`; if it is old, the Pi or its internet is down, so tell the owner rather than resending (a resent command could run twice once the Pi is back).

### Several to-dos at once

To add more than one to-do, do not send them one by one and wait for each reply. `dev/todo-bulk.sh` checks the list, writes all the commands, sends them back to back and prints one line per item. The Pi runs up to 20 commands per poll, so they all land within about 10-20 seconds. The list reads top-down in the order you give. Add `--dry-run` to see the commands without sending.

```sh
printf '%s' '[{"title":"Call the dentist","level":"must","due":"2026-11-20"},"Buy milk","Renew passport"]' | dev/todo-bulk.sh -
```

- `translate.status` (server 0.12.0): Spanish translations of the owner's own text: on/off, lines saved, the daily limit, and new lines, Gemini calls, tokens and estimated cost for today, this month and all time (counts only, never the texts)
- `status`, `ping` (answers with the server version)
- `restart`: the server stops after replying and Docker starts it again with the code already on the Pi. It does not `git pull`; the owner does that on the Pi.

Working signal: before a job that will take more than a few seconds and will end by changing the to-dos (kind `todo`), the calendar (`calendar`) or the A World Worth Saving snapshot (`awws`), send `say {"text": "hub:working <kind> <seconds>"}` with a generous time, for example `say {"text": "hub:working awws 300"}`. The TV then shows a small quiet "working" chip on that tile. It clears itself when the data lands (it turns into the usual "new data" chip when something changed), when the time is up (10 to 900 seconds, 120 if you leave the number out), or when you send `say {"text": "hub:working <kind> done"}`, which you should do when the job ends with no change (a calendar refresh or a to-do list that finds nothing new sends the TV nothing, so only `done` or the time ends it). Only use this once the TV runs a hub version that knows the signal (v0.28.7 or later; check the About panel): an older hub shows the text as a message on the screen.

Motion: if the TV's About panel says `extras off (slow screen)` and the owner wants to see the chips or the character anyway, send `say {"text": "hub:motion push"}` (that session only; a reload goes back to auto). `say {"text": "hub:motion auto"}` forgets the step-down and lets the TV time itself again. Needs hub v0.29.11 or later.

Other calls: list replies `curl -sS "$B?prefix=outbox/"` (an array of `{key, ...}`); delete an object with `curl -sS -X DELETE "$B/inbox%2F$ID.json"` (only to take back a command the Pi has not picked up yet). Results are kept 7 days. No git, docker or shell commands can be sent this way, by design.

### The weekly Pi health report

A script on the Pi (`health/pi-health.py`, systemd user timer, Sundays 3:15 AM Central) writes a scrubbed JSON report that the hub copies to the bucket. Read it with the same curl: `curl -sS "$B/health%2Flatest.json"` (last week's: `health%2FYYYY-MM-DD.json`). `status.json` carries `health: {generated, overall, bad, warn}`. Start with `summary` and `flags` (each `{level: bad|warn, area, text}`), then the section a flag names; compare with the previous dated report for trends. The mailbox cannot run the script (no shell, by design); a fresh report on demand means asking on the Pi: `python3 health/pi-health.py`. The thresholds are `LIMITS` in the script. New checks go in `health/checks.d/` (see its README), never as secrets or addresses in the output.

## Rules

- Never print, copy, echo or paste the token or the `HUB_MAILBOX_` keys, and never read `.env` aloud. `./hub` reads it for you.
- `./hub token` is only for first setup. If it says a token already exists, leave it.
- "No screen is listening" is not an error: the TV is off or Home Hub is closed. Say so plainly.
- If the server is not running, try `docker compose up -d` from this folder, then `./hub status`. If it still fails, show the last lines of `docker compose logs hub` (they never contain the token).
- Never print, copy, echo or paste `SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET` or anything in `data/spotify.json`.
- Never print, copy, echo or paste `HA_TOKEN`, and never put Home Assistant entity ids, `HA_URL` or `WLED_URL` in a message, a commit or a public file: they belong only in `.env` and `data/ambience.json` on the Pi.
- Never print, copy, echo or paste `HA_TOKEN` (the Home Assistant token), and never log what plays (titles, speaker names) or what was asked for on an Echo (the `--query` text).
- Never show, print, copy or log a calendar link (the `CALENDAR_*_URL` lines in `.env`). They are secret keys to the owner's calendars. If one may have leaked, tell the owner to reset it in Google Calendar (README.md, "If a secret address leaks").
- To put an event on the TV, add it through the Google Calendar connector, then run `./hub calendar refresh`. Google may take a while to include it; if it is not there yet, say so and try again in a few minutes.
- Do not edit `data/state.json` by hand while the server runs; use `./hub todo ...`.
- The A World Worth Saving snapshot must never contain email addresses, links, notes text or librarians' last names. The Pi refuses a section with an email address or a link, but it cannot spot notes or names, so leave them out yourself. The owner said yes to money figures on the TV on 2026-11-16, so the `money` section may be sent. To keep money off the TV (for guests, say), set `showMoney: false` under `widgets.awws` in `app/config.js`; the Pi still stores the section.
- The budget snapshot carries totals only: never a balance, a transaction list or an amount on an alert, and never email addresses or links. The owner said yes on 2026-11-17 to the names of his top 5 merchants with their month-to-date totals (the `merchants` section) and to the runway sent as headroom (checking minus what the cards owe minus the floor), never checking or a card balance by itself. Do not read budget figures out of `data/state.json` or send them anywhere; `./hub budget show` is enough to say whether it is up to date. To hide the budget on the TV, set `show: false` under `widgets.budget` in `app/config.js`.
- The show list is the owner's own; change it only when asked. Hulu titles open in Disney+ on the TV by default (`watchAliases` under `widgets.entertainment` in `app/config.js`); give a show `alsoOn` when the owner says it is on another app too. The schedule comes from TVmaze (public, no key); there is no TMDB key on this Pi.
- Never print, copy, echo or paste the TV remote token (`data/tvremote.json`) or the TV's address. A run's link (`payload`) is never put in a log line.
- Logs must never name a show, an episode title, a link, a token or an app title: the Pi's own lines only count (`shows refresh: 4 of 4 shows read`, `screen marked an episode watched`, `screen took a watched mark off an episode`, `screen marked a show caught up`). Keep it that way in anything you add.
- Setup and troubleshooting are in `README.md` in this folder.
