# Home Hub: what shows at each tile size

This mirrors `SIZE_MATRIX` in the Layout Studio artifact (English text only; the artifact also has Spanish). Checked against the TV code in PR 19 (v0.13.0), and Now Playing at v0.15.0 (`app/hub.js` `sizeOf`/`applySize`, `app/style.css` tile sizes, `app/widgets/*.js` and `*.css`). The A World Worth Saving rows were checked against v0.17.0 (`app/widgets/awws.js` and `awws.css`), Earned vs spent and the budget tiles (`app/widgets/budget.js`) at v0.28.4; a budget tile with no data says “Waiting for the budget app”. These four money tiles follow the hub's Language (v0.28.5): in Spanish their titles are Ganado y gastado, Margen diario, Pago de tarjeta and Metas, their words and dates are Spanish (“de $60 al día · quedan 9 días”, “jue 19 nov · en 3 días”, “Cubierto”, “Faltan $120”, “Cargo nuevo: …”), amounts keep the $1,265 form, and goal names and texts, transfer labels and merchants stay as the budget app sends them. A word too long for a 3-column strip (Card day) is shown alone, shrunk to fit. When their data is missing, each of those tiles says why instead (Not synced yet, Needs the Pi, No “School Visits” calendar on the Pi, School Visits couldn’t be read), and “as of 7:10 AM” under the title when the data is old.

## Rules

- The grid is 12 columns by 6 rows. A tile's size in cells picks how it draws.
- **Tier (height):** strip = 1 row, standard = 2 rows, tall = 3 or more rows.
- **Width:** narrow = 1–2 columns, standard = 3–5 columns, wide = 6 or more. A 1-column tile also gets `tile-w1`: no words, only the one number, time or icon that fits.
- **Clicked** opens the panel (46 × 26 rem on an 80 × 45 rem screen, about a third of the screen); its Full screen button fills the screen. Every widget opens, the Clock included: since v0.13.0 its tile takes focus and OK opens the Clock page. (A widget with `expandable: false` would be a static tile that focus skips; none uses it now.)
